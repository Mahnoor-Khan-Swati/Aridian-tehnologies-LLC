About HBL AI Assistant

This assistant uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation to answer banking questions from the provided HBL documents. Simple greetings are answered instantly; banking questions use HBL document retrieval, an LLM and local text-to-speech.

This is an educational demonstration. Confirm official information at www.hbl.com or an HBL branch. It does not access personal accounts or perform transactions.
